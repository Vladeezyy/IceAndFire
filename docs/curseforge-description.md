# Ice and Fire (Unofficial Port)

**Dragons, sea serpents, myrmex hives and the dread creatures, on NeoForge and Fabric for the new Minecraft versions.**

## 📜 Original mod

This is an unofficial port of **[Ice and Fire: Dragons](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/ice-and-fire-dragons)**
2.1.13 by **Alexthe666 and TheBv** (Forge 1.20.1). Original source:
[github.com/AlexModGuy/Ice_and_Fire](https://github.com/AlexModGuy/Ice_and_Fire), license **LGPL-3.0**; this port is
LGPL-3.0 too. All the content, art, sounds and ideas are theirs; please report bugs of this port to
[this port's issues](https://github.com/Vladeezyy/IceAndFire/issues), not to the original authors.

### How this port differs from the original

- **New Minecraft versions and loaders:** NeoForge **and Fabric** for Minecraft **1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2 and
  26.3** (the original is Forge 1.20.1 only).
- **Same content:** every dragon, creature, item, block and structure of 2.1.13, the bestiary and the dragon forge. Worlds and items of the 1.20.1 version are upgraded.
- **[Rampart](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rampart) instead of Citadel:** the model and animation
  classes of Citadel that the mod uses, ported to every version and loader; models draw exactly as with Citadel.
- **Bugs of the original fixed**, among them: dragons drawn several times too big with a 23-block hitbox on 1.21 (the
  rider sat under the body), dragon parts piling up in old worlds ("UUID of added entity already exists"), features
  generated at the world border, the myrmex queen leaving her hive, hippogryphs stuck in the air, ~200 useless network
  packets per second per dragon, clients able to damage any entity or rewrite any hive through the network. The full
  list is in the [README](https://github.com/Vladeezyy/IceAndFire#fixed-bugs-of-the-original).
- **Tested:** server self-tests, a load test with 30 adult dragons and 100 creatures, a dedicated server with a client
  riding a dragon, and a client scene with every creature, also together with Sodium, Iris, Lithium, FerriteCore, Jade
  and EntityCulling.

## 🐉 About the mod

Ice and Fire brings the creatures of myth into Minecraft. Find fire, ice and lightning dragons in their caves and roosts,
raise one from an egg, ride it and dress it in armor. Forge dragonsteel from the blood and bones of slain dragons, face
the hydra, the cyclops, gorgons, sea serpents and the dread undead, and record every creature in the bestiary.

- **Dragons:** three kinds, five growth stages, caves and roosts with their hoards, tamed dragons that fight and carry you
- **Creatures:** hippogryphs, amphitheres, hippocampi, pixies, sirens, trolls, cyclopes, death worms, cockatrices,
  Stymphalian birds, ghosts, myrmex hives with their queen, and more
- **Gear:** dragon scale armor, dragonsteel, dragon bone weapons, the dragon forge, the bestiary

## 📦 Installation

Pick the file for your Minecraft version and loader. Needed on both the client and the server.

- **Minecraft 1.21.1:** NeoForge 21.1.197+ or Fabric (Fabric API)
- **Minecraft 1.21.11:** NeoForge 21.11.48+ or Fabric (Fabric API)
- **Minecraft 26.1.2:** NeoForge 26.1.2.121+ or Fabric (Fabric API)
- **Minecraft 26.2:** NeoForge 26.2.0.93+ or Fabric (Fabric API)
- **Minecraft 26.3:** NeoForge 26.3.0.99+ or Fabric (Fabric API)
- **Required:** [Rampart](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/rampart) **1.1.0+** for the same version and
  loader, and [Fabric API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/fabric-api) on Fabric
- **Optional:** [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei) (the dragon forge recipes)

This is a **beta**. Source, changelog and older files: [github.com/Vladeezyy/IceAndFire](https://github.com/Vladeezyy/IceAndFire).
